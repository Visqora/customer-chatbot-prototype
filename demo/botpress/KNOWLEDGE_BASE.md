## Store Overview

Northstar Apparel is an online fashion store selling everyday shirts, trousers, jackets, and accessories. The chatbot may answer only from this document. It cannot access customer accounts, live stock, payments, couriers, or order systems.

## Shipping

### Delivery locations

Northstar Apparel ships to serviceable addresses within India. International shipping is not available.

### Processing time

Orders are prepared within 1-2 business days. Business days exclude Sundays and public holidays.

### Delivery time

Standard delivery usually takes 3-7 business days after dispatch. Delivery times are estimates and may be affected by the destination, weather, public holidays, or courier delays.

### Shipping charge

Standard shipping is free for orders of ₹999 or more. A ₹79 shipping charge applies to orders below ₹999.

### Order tracking

Customers normally receive tracking details after dispatch. The chatbot cannot access an order or verify live tracking. For order-specific help, contact visqora@gmail.com.

### Address changes

The chatbot cannot change a delivery address. A customer may email visqora@gmail.com immediately, but an address change is not guaranteed after an order has been placed or dispatched.

## Returns

### Return window

Eligible products may be requested for return within 14 calendar days of delivery.

### Return conditions

Returned products must be unused, unwashed, unworn except for a reasonable fit check, and returned with original tags and packaging. The customer should retain proof of purchase.

### Non-returnable products

The following products are not returnable unless received damaged or incorrect:

- Final-sale products.
- Gift cards.
- Personal-care products.
- Innerwear and other hygiene-sensitive products.
- Products marked non-returnable on the product page.

### Return process

To request a return, email visqora@gmail.com with the order reference, product name, and reason. Do not include payment-card information or passwords. The support team must confirm eligibility before a product is sent back.

### Refund timing

After an approved return is received and inspected, the refund is initiated to the original payment method within 5-7 business days. A bank or payment provider may require additional processing time.

### Shipping charges and refunds

Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the product was damaged, defective, or incorrect. Contact support to confirm whether a return pickup fee applies.

## Exchanges

Eligible apparel may be exchanged for a different available size within 14 calendar days of delivery. Exchange products must satisfy the same condition requirements as returns.

Exchanges depend on size availability when the request is processed. The chatbot cannot reserve inventory or guarantee an exchange. If the requested size is unavailable, the support team may offer a return under the approved return policy.

To request an exchange, email visqora@gmail.com. The chatbot cannot approve or complete an exchange.

## Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Products

Customers should contact visqora@gmail.com within 48 hours of delivery when a product arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect. The message should include the order reference and clear photos of the product and packaging. Do not send payment information or passwords.

The support team reviews each report before confirming replacement, exchange, or refund eligibility. The chatbot cannot guarantee the outcome.

## Cancellations

The chatbot cannot cancel orders. A customer may email visqora@gmail.com immediately after ordering. Cancellation is not guaranteed after packing or dispatch.
