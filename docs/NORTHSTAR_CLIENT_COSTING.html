06 · Assumptions & safeguards

Cost controls and responsibilities

Transparent by design

Pricing assumptions

  • One English-language bot and one commercial website.
  • Up to 5 readable sources and 15 acceptance questions.
  • One standard Botpress webchat embed with no custom backend.
  • One consolidated revision round before launch.
  • Client content is final, accurate, and legally approved.
  • Provider subscriptions and usage are client-paid.

Usage controls

  • Client reviews Botpress conversation and AI usage.
  • Botpress paid plans can automatically add conversation packs.
  • Vercel Pro can bill usage beyond included credit.
  • Enable provider alerts and spend-management controls.
  • No paid add-on is enabled by Visqora without approval.
  • Provider price changes pass through at actual cost.
Important Botpress billing behavior: the official pricing FAQ states that paid accounts receive warnings as usage rises and can automatically purchase 100-conversation packs near the quota. AI credit grants may also auto-recharge at the provider threshold. The client should monitor billing and right-size the plan.

Warranty versus new scope

A covered defect is reproducible behavior that fails an accepted requirement using the same approved content, configuration, website environment, and platform capabilities. New features, changed policies, provider outages, client changes, new integrations, or usage-limit exhaustion are not warranty defects.

Validity and provider variability

This professional-service quote is valid for 15 calendar days. Third-party prices are research snapshots, not fixed resale prices. Botpress, Vercel, domain registrars, tax authorities, banks, and currency markets may change the final recurring charge.

Approval

Approval confirms the fixed scope, assumptions, exclusions, milestone payments, client-owned provider billing, and change-control process described in this proposal.
